Overview

Program Overview

The Master of Science in Information Technology (MSIT) at IIIT Hyderabad is being reimagined for an AI-native world.

Founded by Professor Raj Reddy, MSIT has, for over 25 years, followed a distinctive learning-by-doing philosophy, with project-based learning, close mentoring, and a strong emphasis on professional skills.

The rebuilt MSIT is designed to prepare students not merely for the technologies and jobs of today, but to continuously learn, adapt, solve problems, and create value throughout their careers.

Curriculum

Curriculum

The curriculum is being redesigned for a world in which AI and cognitive technologies are ubiquitous. It combines strong technical foundations with the ability to learn and apply emerging technologies.

Programming and computational problem solving, data science and machine learning, artificial intelligence, deep learning, modern AI systems and large language models, software and systems development, applied projects, industry problem solving, entrepreneurship and product development, communication and professional skills, critical thinking and responsible use of AI.

Across the curriculum, students will be expected to use AI tools, evaluate their outputs, and understand when human reasoning and judgement are required. Detailed course titles, credits, sequencing, and electives are still to be published.
