About

About the Programme

The Master of Science in Information Technology (MSIT) is a two-year, full-time, on-campus postgraduate programme designed to help students build strong technical foundations, hands-on project experience and deeper expertise in information technology.

The programme combines conceptual learning with practical application, enabling students to work on real-world problems while developing strong programming, software development and problem-solving skills.

Duration 2 Years
Mode Full-time / On-campus
Location IIIT Hyderabad
Degree Master of Science in Information Technology
Learning by Doing

The programme focuses on practical learning, projects, problem solving and application of technical concepts in real-world contexts.
